      Love, Share, Invite: A Menu of Missionary Opportunities. Everyone can participate.
      Start with what feels natural! Our Shared Purpose: As we gather Israel on both sides
      of the veil and prepare for the Savior's return, we help build His kingdom. In the
      leadership session of stake conference, President Kloepfer highlighted Elder Renlund's
      "Menu of Missionary Opportunities" as a way for every ward member to find natural
      opportunities to love, share, and invite. The goal is not another assignment; it is a
      ward culture in which everyone recognizes opportunities to act with love and
      confidence. That lasting change begins with small, sincere efforts. Love: Build
      Christlike relationships: welcome someone new, serve a neighbor, and nurture genuine
      friendships. Share: Share what brings joy: a gospel blessing, an uplifting scripture,
      a meaningful experience, or your testimony. Invite: Invite others to experience the
      gospel: attend church, join a ward activity, or meet with the missionaries. A
      Prophetic Invitation: President Dallin H. Oaks taught that the Lord's truth is coming
      forth to gather His children and testify that Jesus is the Christ. He invited all to
      share these truths so that more people may draw closer to the Savior. Adapted from
      "The Gospel of Jesus Christ Is for All," October 2026 General Conference. Small acts.
      Genuine love. Meaningful invitations. Everyone can do something!
