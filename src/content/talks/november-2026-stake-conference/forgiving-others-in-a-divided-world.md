      The Savior's Example. How the Savior Forgave Others. He Showed Us How. "Father, forgive
      them; for they know not what they do." Luke 23:34, forgiving the men who crucified Him.

  - type: graphic
    image: ./images/haymore-slide-08.jpg
    alt: >-
      Why It's Hard. Why do we struggle with Forgiving Others? 1. Often the deepest hurts come
      from those we know best, from those who we expect to know better. 2. We forget that the
      progress of others is still pending. 3. We want justice for those who have harmed us.
      Beside a portrait of Elder Jeffrey R. Holland: "Let people repent. Let people grow.
      Believe that people can change and improve." Elder Jeffrey R. Holland, "Remember Lot's
      Wife," BYU devotional, 2009.

      Beside a portrait of Sister Kristin M. Yee: "I came to realize in a profound way that

  - type: graphic

  - type: graphic
    image: ./images/haymore-slide-11.jpg
    alt: >-
      Honest About the Hard Cases. Forgiving the Deepest Hurts. What it is and isn't.
      Forgiveness IS: Praying and seeking to forgive others gives you divine strength, and
      opens the doors of repentance to the one who hurt you. Trusting the Savior that He will
      make up what you have lost. Something you can do while also setting healthy boundaries
      for the future. Forgiveness ISN'T: Weakness. It is calling upon the strength of the
      Savior. Choosing for them. They must still choose to trust the Savior and to repent. An
      endless opportunity to open ourselves to more hurt.
